LivingEvidenceMap full-text article coding prompt
Schema version: fulltext_coding_v2
Prompt version: fulltext_coding_v3

TASK
Code the supplied full text into the current LivingEvidenceMap JSON schema. This prompt and the machine-readable schema are the sole coding authority. Extract from the supplied article itself. Return exactly one valid JSON object with no commentary or extra fields.

CURRENT SUBSTANTIVE FIELDS
Exactly these 26 fields:
document_type, review_type, study_type, study_design, research_approach, setting, sample_unit, study_period, location_region, location_country, species, other_farmed_species, study_population, aquaculture_facility, system_studied, production_stage, fish_life_stage, exposure_intervention, comparator, outcome_measured, funding_body, research_question, objectives_summary, ontology_codes, multiple_studies_flag, multiple_studies_reason.

In addition, return exactly these schema-defined audit structures: evidence, document_completeness, document_completeness_evidence, field_completeness, not_reported_fields. Return no other keys, metadata or workflow provenance.

EVIDENCE
Every populated substantive field requires a field-specific evidence entry. Evidence must be actual article text or a faithful extract/context from the supplied full text supporting that exact field/value. Never put model-generated reasoning, interpretation or conclusions into evidence. If a value is inferred, provide the underlying source text; put the inference only in the coded value. Do not use model knowledge or cited studies as evidence. Evidence must refer to the study being coded, not background or a different cited study.

For primary empirical studies, Methods/Materials and Methods and Results/Findings control empirical/methodological variables; search them thoroughly, including tables and figure legends. Other sections may be used where expressly permitted below. For non-primary documents, code the document's own explicit content and do not construct an empirical study from cited studies.

ELIGIBLE SPECIES
Eligible species are Atlantic salmon, chum salmon, pink salmon, coho salmon, chinook salmon, sockeye salmon, masu salmon, rainbow trout, and unspecified salmon species. Keep eligible and ineligible components separate. Never transfer characteristics, methods, populations, settings, locations, exposures, comparators or outcomes between them. other_farmed_species is a free-text array for additional farmed species actually part of the eligible document/system context.

DOCUMENT TYPE / REVIEW
document_type: study, review, perspective, commentary, editorial, book, book_chapter, report, thesis, protocol, other.
review_type: systematic_style when explicitly a systematic/systematic-style review or equivalent methodology/quantitative synthesis is described; otherwise primer_overview for reviews; not_applicable otherwise.
For non-primary documents without their own eligible empirical study, do not invent empirical study population, sample unit, period, design, comparator or outcome from cited studies.

STUDY TYPE / DESIGN / APPROACH
study_type: experimental, observational, modelling, not_stated, not_applicable. Experimental requires deliberate manipulation/imposition on eligible subjects/specimens; laboratory processing of field samples alone is not experimental. Non-primary documents without their own eligible empirical study use not_applicable.
study_design: BA, CI, BACI, RCT, Time-series, Modelling, Qualitative, not_stated, not_applicable. CI is for genuine intervention/treatment comparisons, including deliberately imposed alternative challenge modes. Do not invent design labels.
research_approach: quantitative, qualitative, mixed_methods, not_applicable.

SETTING
Only field, laboratory/controlled facility, in_vitro, in_silico. For primary studies, setting is where the substantive eligible-species study occurred, not later sample processing. Field includes real farms, sea cages, hatcheries, rivers, lakes and environmental locations, including controlled field experiments. Controlled tanks/research-facility aquaculture experiments are laboratory/controlled facility. Laboratory processing of field samples does not make the study laboratory.

SAMPLE UNIT
Use one primary high-level methodological unit when clearly identifiable (e.g. fish, cells, farms, communities, grab samples, households). Do not reconstruct nesting, pseudoreplication, statistical units, technical subsamples, parasites, tissues, assays, derived measurements or tanks merely used as containers. If fish are the primary experimental unit, prefer fish over downstream tissues/assays. Sample size is not an extraction variable and must not be calculated or inferred.

LOCATION
For primary studies use explicit study-setting information from Methods/study setting and relevant Results. Do not confuse origin of a pathogen, strain, feed ingredient, cited study or other material with study location unless explicitly connected. For non-primary documents, explicit eligible system location anywhere may support region/country.

AQUACULTURE FACILITY
Only salmon_farming_region, hatchery, open_cages, closed_cages, land_based, land_based_RAS. Code only an explicitly stated eligible salmon production facility/system in which the study occurred or that is explicitly evaluated. hatchery requires explicit hatchery production context; open_cages includes sea pens/open net pens/sea cages unless explicitly closed; closed_cages requires explicit closed sides/walls or equivalent; land_based requires explicit land-based aquaculture not identified as RAS; land_based_RAS requires explicit land-based RAS; salmon_farming_region requires explicit regional salmon-farming context. Do not infer facility from life stage. Laboratory water circulation is not RAS. Do not force a research station/laboratory into a production-facility category.

SYSTEM / PRODUCTION STAGE
system_studied is the actual biological, environmental, social system or material substantively examined for eligible components. For primary studies describe the actual experimental/observational system, not a broader application/background context, source population, cited study or generic aquaculture context. Do not let Introduction/background override a different Methods-defined experimental system.
production_stage only: Feed, Hatchery, Transfer between Hatchery and Adult, Adult grow-out, Processing. It describes the production context actually studied/evaluated and is independent of fish_life_stage. Feed means feed production/manufacture outside aquaculture; testing diets on fish is not automatically Feed. Hatchery requires relevant hatchery production context. Transfer requires explicit transfer context. Adult grow-out includes explicitly framed grow-out fish and associated wastes/cages/water/sediments. Processing concerns harvested aquaculture product. Do not code Hatchery merely because experimental fish originated there. Do not infer production_stage from generic aquaculture references.

FISH LIFE STAGE
Controlled values only: Sperm, Egg, Embryo, Alevin, Fry, Parr, Pre-smolt, Smolt, Juvenile, Adult, Broodstock, Product.
Harvest is NOT a fish_life_stage value and must never be emitted there; harvest is production-stage/context information only. Search the whole relevant document for explicit life-stage terminology, especially Methods and Results, tables, figures and later stages of the same cohort. Production stage and fish life stage are independent.

LIFE-STAGE INFERENCE
If life stage is not explicit, infer ONLY Juvenile or Adult. Never infer another life stage from age or mass. Use evidence for the actual fish. Prefer: (1) explicit life-stage terminology; (2) explicit developmental transition/maturation; (3) age and/or body mass plus species-specific farmed growth trajectory and production context; (4) age/mass alone only where the species-specific farmed trajectory makes Juvenile/Adult sufficiently supported. Age/mass inference must be supported by Methods/Materials and Methods or equivalent study-design text for the coded fish; do not infer from Introduction, Discussion, abstract, title, cited literature or general knowledge alone. Use a species-specific growth benchmark when the article provides one; never invent a threshold. If insufficient support, use NOT FOUND. Do not call fish Adult merely because they are large, market-sized, harvested, in grow-out or pre-market; Adult requires explicit adult/maturation evidence or sufficiently strong species-specific age/mass evidence. Do not call fish Juvenile merely because they are small. A longitudinal study may return [Juvenile, Adult] when both are supported. Egg, Fry, Parr, Pre-smolt, Smolt, Broodstock and Product must be explicit, not age/mass inferred.

EXPOSURE / INTERVENTION
There is exactly ONE combined field: exposure_intervention. Code the actual eligible-species intervention and/or investigated exposure. Deliberate assigned treatment/manipulation/vaccination/husbandry change/challenge is an intervention. An investigated observational exposure or explicitly characterised exposure context may be coded. Routine rearing, husbandry, sampling and processing are not automatically interventions. Generic environmental mentions are not automatically exposures. For non-primary documents use only the document's own explicit exposure/intervention context.

COMPARATOR
Only the actual focal comparison within eligible empirical evidence. May be null when none exists. Do not treat grouping, stratification, outcome categories, survivor/mortality status, time points, pathogens, treatment components or analytical subgroups as comparators unless they are genuinely the focal comparator. Do not inherit comparators from cited studies.

OUTCOME
outcome_measured contains substantive outcomes actually measured/assessed using study data and reported by the eligible primary study. Use Methods and Results, including tables/figures. Do not code outcomes merely mentioned in title, abstract, Introduction, Discussion, Conclusions or cited literature. For non-primary documents without their own eligible empirical study, do not transfer cited-study outcomes.

FUNDING
funding_body comes only from explicit funding statements, acknowledgements or equivalent attribution. Do not infer it from affiliation, address or publisher.

RESEARCH QUESTION / OBJECTIVES
These are mandatory retrieval targets. Search the entire document for the document's own explicit research question, aim, purpose, objectives, hypothesis or equivalent. Abstract and Introduction are preferred and should be checked early, but are not mandatory sources. Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, tables or other sections may be used when they explicitly state the document's own question/aim/objectives. For non-primary documents use the document's own aims, not cited-study aims. If an explicit question exists, give a faithful concise representation. If no question is phrased as a question but an explicit aim/purpose/objective exists, convert that statement into a concise question without adding content. objectives_summary comes from explicit aims/objectives/purpose statements. Do not mark NOT FOUND merely because the article uses alternative wording such as investigated, compared, evaluated, examined or sought to. Mark NOT FOUND only after actively checking the full document and finding no explicit statement.

ONTOLOGY
ontology_codes must use only valid codes from the repository's current topic ontology. Do not invent codes.

MULTIPLE STUDIES
Set true only with strong positive evidence of two or more independently structured eligible studies/components: e.g. explicitly labelled independent studies, separate study aims with separate methods/results, distinct eligible samples/populations functioning as independent studies, or explicitly combined independent eligible empirical studies. Multiple treatments, factor levels, conditions, time points, assays, outcomes or populations within one coherent design do not by themselves constitute multiple studies. Cited studies discussed in a review/commentary do not make the flag true. If ambiguous, false. When false, multiple_studies_reason must be null; when true, provide a concise evidence-based reason.

DOCUMENT COMPLETENESS
COMPLETE means sufficient text for reasonable-confidence extraction; INCOMPLETE means clearly truncated/corrupted/missing major sections or otherwise unusable; UNCERTAIN means substantial structural/OCR/missing-content problems but enough remains for partial coding. For INCOMPLETE, do not manufacture missing values; extract only directly supported information and explain the limitation in document_completeness_evidence.

FIELD COMPLETENESS
field_completeness is an exception-only audit of current substantive fields. NOT FOUND means applicable information could not be found after active searching. Do not force semantic distinctions between empty/null/NOT FOUND beyond what the schema requires; downstream processing may normalise them. not_reported_fields must contain only current substantive fields and only those marked NOT FOUND. Never use it for metadata.

FINAL SELF-CHECK
Before returning JSON verify: exactly the current substantive fields plus the five audit structures; only exposure_intervention for exposure/intervention; no sample-size extraction; no Harvest in fish_life_stage; age/mass inference only Juvenile/Adult; every populated substantive field has source-derived field-specific evidence; system_studied describes the actual study system; research question/objectives were searched throughout the full text; comparator is genuine; funding is explicit; multiple_studies_reason follows its flag. Return only the JSON object.
