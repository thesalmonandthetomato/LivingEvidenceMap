Living Evidence Map

Research evidence on salmon farming, at a glance

The Living Evidence Map is a scoping review of research articles on aquaculture of all salmon species, including Atlantic and Pacific salmon and rainbow trout, across all production stages. The map was originally populated by manual screening of >19,000 records and is now maintained using validated machine screening and content annotation, supported by LLMs (OpenAI GPT-5-mini). For further information on the wider project, see The Salmon and the Tomato. This project was coordinated by Dr Neal Haddaway.
